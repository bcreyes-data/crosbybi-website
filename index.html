What usually brings people here

The monthly report takes days

Someone exports, cleans and pastes the same data every week or month, and the report is out of date by the time it is finished.

The numbers don't match

Data lives in spreadsheets, software and different departments, so two people can bring two different totals to the same meeting.

Decisions wait on the data

Budget overruns and trends show up late, because nobody can see actuals against plan until the reporting is done.
